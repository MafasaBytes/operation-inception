# MS04.2 — Deterministic rules engine

## Research question and status

Can the approved 6-column × 9-row board support a deterministic, auditable
multi-competitor rule system whose captures, cascades, elimination, and winner
are genuine consequences of legal moves rather than authored animation?

The pure rules implementation and conformance scenarios are owner-approved and
now supply game truth to MS04.3. This milestone produces no video or audio and
does not claim that the game is yet compelling to watch.

## Locked rules

### Players and turns

- A game has two to eight unique player IDs in a fixed turn order.
- A player may place one orb in an empty cell or a cell they already own.
  Placement in an opponent-owned cell is illegal.
- Eliminated players are skipped. No move is accepted after a win or draw.
- The canonical product board remains 9 rows × 6 columns, using the zero-based
  `(row, column)` and north/east/south/west topology approved in MS04.1.

### Critical mass and ownership

- Critical mass equals orthogonal neighbor count: corner 2, edge 3, interior 4.
- Reaching critical mass queues an explosion.
- An explosion subtracts exactly that cell's critical mass and sends one orb to
  each orthogonal neighbor in north, east, south, west order, omitting directions
  outside the board.
- Every incoming orb converts its receiving cell immediately to the exploding
  player's ownership, while retaining the cell's existing orbs and adding one.
- Excess is never discarded. One critical mass is subtracted per explosion; any
  residual remains in the source cell. A still-critical residual is queued again.

### Cascade ordering and depth

- Explosions resolve through a FIFO queue. A cell already waiting in the queue is
  not duplicated; additional incoming orbs accumulate before its queued turn.
- The first explosion caused by placement has depth 0. A newly critical neighbor
  is queued at its triggering explosion's depth plus one.
- A queued cell uses its current owner when it explodes. This makes an intervening
  capture visible in the logical outcome rather than preserving a stale attacker.
- Public game states are always settled: every orb count is below that cell's
  critical mass. Rendering and sound therefore never need to finish game logic.

### Elimination and victory

- Opening protection ends only after every original competitor has completed at
  least one legal turn.
- After protection ends, elimination is evaluated only after the complete cascade
  settles. A player with zero owned cells is eliminated.
- A winner is declared only after settlement when exactly one active player
  remains. Elimination and victory events follow all explosion/capture events.
- The rules engine never selects, scripts, or favors a winner.

### Non-termination

- The engine fingerprints the complete board and pending FIFO queue to detect a
  repeated cascade state.
- A configurable explosion-step ceiling is also enforced; the default is 10,000.
- A repeated state or ceiling produces an explicit draw. The attempted move is
  rolled back to the last settled board and the returned event list contains only
  `turn_started` followed by `cascade_aborted` with the precise reason.
- A truncated cascade can never emit elimination or `winner_declared`.

## Immutable logical events

Every successful move returns a new frozen `GameState` and a frozen sequence of
events with global contiguous sequence numbers and stable IDs. Events have no
seconds or frame numbers.

| Event | Logical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `turn_started` | Current player began a legal turn attempt |
| `orb_placed` | Placement incremented an empty or owned cell |
| `cell_charged` | Settled count remains below critical mass |
| `cell_became_critical` | Count crossed the critical threshold |
| `cell_exploded` | One critical mass was subtracted; `orb_count` is the residual |
| `orb_received` | Every distributed orb reached a neighbor, including same-owner arrivals |
| `neighbor_captured` | Incoming ownership differed, including an empty cell |
| `cascade_depth_increased` | First resolved explosion at a deeper level |
| `player_eliminated` | Player owns zero cells after protected settlement |
| `winner_declared` | Sole active player after protected settlement |
| `cascade_aborted` | Cycle or step ceiling caused rollback and explicit draw |

The names intentionally overlap MS05 where semantics agree, but the rules module
does not import audio. A later presentation-time adapter must map a **complete**
logical log to absolute seconds before MS05 can consume it.

## Conformance package

`fixtures/ms04.2/rules-v1.json` contains two short, hand-authored legal scenarios:

1. `corner-capture-victory` verifies corner threshold, stable neighbor order,
   immediate conversion, post-settlement elimination, and winner timing.
2. `two-depth-cascade` verifies a depth-0 corner explosion queuing a depth-1 edge
   explosion while both competitors remain active.

These are synthetic rules examples, not automated matches or content candidates.
Running the fixture creates `outputs/ms04.2/ms04.2-conformance-v1.json`, containing
the legal moves, complete semantic events, final sparse board states, locked rule
decisions, and source/fixture hashes.

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m inception.ms04_2 \
  --fixture fixtures/ms04.2/rules-v1.json \
  --output outputs/ms04.2/ms04.2-conformance-v1.json
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_ms04_2.py
.venv/bin/python tools/verify_particle_freeze.py
```

The exporter refuses an existing output unless `--overwrite` is supplied. That
flag applies only to the MS04.2 conformance JSON.

## What technical checks do and do not establish

Checks cover legal placement, two-to-eight competitors, topology thresholds,
neighbor and FIFO ordering, depth, immediate ownership, excess conservation,
opening protection, eliminated-turn skipping, post-settlement victory, stable
event identity, rollback draws, immutable settled states, and deterministic
fixture reproduction.

They do not establish strategic depth, spectator comprehension, anticipation,
fairness, match length, comeback frequency, or social-video attention. Those need
genuine automated matches in MS04.3 and later human review. No renderer, audio
adapter, policy, seed, model, voiceover, publishing integration, or branded game
interface is added here.
