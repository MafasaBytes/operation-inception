## State, identities, and geometry

x=0..W at actual height H. Nominal W=1, original H0=2.2, reference r0=.08,
24 initially stationary balls, two or three equally treated colored lineages.
The later phone composition must retain bottom/right safe clearance while making
the chamber dominant. There is no renderer in MS06.2.

A ball retains its lineage through every split; there is no capture or conversion.
Persistent root identities are `r000` etc. Children append `.0` and `.1`, retain
parent identity and parent generation, and **advance generation by one**. Roots
are g0; g3 cannot split. Radius at generation g is r0×.75^g. IDs are never reused.
A retired split parent is ancestry, not a crushed ball. Escaped balls remain alive
and count toward the cap. Birth/death time is supplied by its structural event;
retired records also retain death tick.

history, drive, and clocks. The seed is recorded provenance: contacts and rules

## Stylized motion and deterministic packing

This is overdamped disk choreography, not hydraulic engineering or material
physics. There is **no gravity or spontaneous wandering** in compression.
Piston motion and ordered projection move disks. The clock is fixed at
**120 Hz**, independent of video FPS, sound, or wall-clock runtime.

At each tick, trial the commanded height. Resolve at most **64 projection passes**,
in boundary order piston, floor, left, right (balls sorted by persistent ID), then
lexicographically sorted unordered ball pairs. Boundary penetration pushes inward;
pair penetration is shared equally along the center normal. Coincident centers
use a horizontal normal, lower ID left. Revisit in the same order until maximum
penetration <= **epsilon=1e-6**, or exhaust the budget. No geometry is randomized.

A failed piston trial rolls back its proposed movement and packing: retain the
last actual height, relax any permitted birth overlap there, and record a stall.
A stall is neither containment nor automatically invalid. Compression velocities
are committed displacement×120; new children initially have zero velocity.
All finite state and contact constraints remain auditable in tick samples.

A split starts children at parent center ± **.65×parent radius** along the
perpendicular to strongest compression. Center separation is **1.30×parent
radius**, each child radius **.75×parent radius**. The intentional sibling
penetration is **.20×parent radius**, or 26.6667% of child radius. Children are
independent during the same ordered projection; no tangent positions are injected.
The event records proposed and relaxed positions, passes, initial/residual overlap.

Permitted residual overlap is bounded: maximum penetration divided by the smallest
inside radius must be <= **.50**; a larger unresolved penetration is invalid.
Finite residual overlap above epsilon has at most **30 consecutive ticks / .25 s**
to relax, otherwise a `finite_packing_stall` draw. This permits temporary sibling
overlap without silently accepting severe penetration. Initial overlaps above
epsilon are invalid; impossible births cannot be hidden by shrinking/deleting balls.
A failed state's diagnostics remain explicit and are never renderable content.

## Auditable normalized load and wall stress

Contacts use committed geometry and epsilon. A ball pair counts once; each side
wall counts once even at a panel junction; piston and floor count separately.
Construct the undirected contact graph and mark its piston-connected component.

    C = 1 if connected to the engaged piston, else 0
    travel_after_first_contact = first_contact_height - actual_height
    D = clamp(travel_after_first_contact / drive_ramp_distance, 0, 1)
    Q = neighbors + 2*piston + side_walls + floor
    L = C * D * min(1, Q / 4)

First contact records tick, time and **actual** piston height. D is exactly zero
there. Only further actual travel increases it; a stalled command cannot increase
D. The versioned default ramp is **2*r0=.16**; every conformance scenario records
.16 explicitly. Graph disconnection sets C=0 even if the global travel drive is
high. Record C, D, neighbor IDs, boundary indicators, Q (`weight`) and L separately.
No initial drive/load/clock is supplied by a fixture. D rises continuously with
travel; changing contacts can still change L discontinuously.

The strongest compression axis is the largest-eigenvalue axis of Σw*n*nᵀ from
contact normals with the same weights. Eigenvalue ties within 1e-9 use vertical.
Choose the perpendicular with positive x, or positive y when vertical; `.0` uses
its negative direction. This tensor is a geometric rule, not a physical stress.

**The complete PB-1.0 wall-stress formula is preserved:** four fixed panels per
side, `L0..L3` and `R0..R3`, each height H0/4, bottom upward. Assign a touching
ball by center y, lower-inclusive/upper-exclusive (last upper endpoint included).
Floor and piston cannot rupture. For a closed panel s:

    W_s = min(1, sum(L_i for wall contacts assigned to s) / 2)

Two unit-load contacts saturate wall stress. This proxy is dimensionless, not
material strength. A ruptured panel opens its exact logged interval.

## Thresholds, clocks, and latched ordering

| Condition | Consecutive duration | Consequence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| .50 <= L < .90 | .30 s / 36 complete intervals | Eligible parent splits |
| L >= .90 | .15 s / 18 complete intervals | Ball is crushed |
| W >= .60 | Immediate band entry | Wall strained indication |
| W >= .80 | .40 s / 48 complete intervals | Panel ruptures |
| Stable final packing, no pending enabled clocks | .60 s / 72 intervals | Containment |
| Rupture release | .60 s / 72 intervals | Rupture resolution |

Comparisons use 1e-12 numerical boundary tolerance; geometry uses its separate
epsilon. A newly observed condition starts at zero. Increment only when the
**whole condition qualifies at both consecutive ticks**, otherwise reset. Extreme
load resets moderate time. Generation 3 and live-cap disable/reset split clocks;
freeing capacity requires a new complete moderate interval, never a backlog.

Each tick resolves motion, then calculates contacts, loads, wall stress, and
clocks from one immutable pre-transition snapshot. Latch every completed
qualification. Apply **crush → rupture → split**, each cohort sorted by
qualification tick then persistent identity (all newly due entries share the tick).
Events are contiguous sequence-numbered and carry stable run/sequence identities.

A crush removes actual area and contacts. Later ticks can have lower pressure,
but a same-tick latched rupture **cannot be canceled by that relief**. One panel,
the first qualifying panel in stable order, opens. Rupture stops subsequent
splits. Latched splits remain qualified despite other same-tick births; capacity
is checked before each atomic parent-to-two-child replacement. Cap-blocked parents
remain intact. No renderer or lineage preference participates in these decisions.

Each split increases area exactly 12.5%: 2×(.75)^2=**1.125**. Rapid multiplication
can create extreme contact traps that crush that lineage and genuinely release
space for another. All lineages obey identical rules; surviving a reversal is not
a fabricated team victory. Fragments or waves may later illustrate logged events,
but cannot create, remove, or recolor balls.

## Bounds and terminal-outcome matrix

Hard maxima: generation **3**, live balls **96** (inside plus escaped), semantic
events **20,000** including a reserved diagnostic slot, **18 s / 2,160 ticks**.
Conformance can lower event/time budgets to exercise draws, never raise maxima.
Positions, velocities, radii, loads and clocks must remain finite; radii positive.

Non-progress: after the final command time, count **240 identical eligible ticks**
using IDs/poses rounded to 1e-9, load/contact audit, actual/command height and mode.
Do not count while a threshold or containment clock advances, or during release.
Inert packing above final height can draw. More complex cycles remain bounded by
event/time limits. Event-budget failure rolls back the whole attempted tick and
uses its reserved final draw record: no partial crush/split cohort is published.

| Outcome | Exact rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Rupture** | A latched panel completes .40 s at W>=.80. Lock rupture mode, open the specific panel, freeze actual piston height, reset all split/crush/rupture clocks and vent loads to zero. No new qualifications. Surviving members of the qualifying pre-transition piston-connected component whose full vertical extent fits the opening receive an outward horizontal velocity .40 unit/s; others zero. Release uses normal deterministic movement/projection against remaining boundaries and pairs. A genuine escape requires the entire disk to cross the open side (left x+r<=0, right x-r>=W), while fitting its vertical opening. Resolve after exactly .60 s; remaining inside balls are retained honestly. Escaped living balls continue outward motion. |
| **Containment** | Actual height reaches that scenario's final target within epsilon, no enabled qualifying condition remains pending, packing stays stable and speeds <=.005 for .60 s. Changes reset the hold. A jam above target cannot count. |
| **Extinction** | Every live ball is genuinely crushed; no escaped survivors. Record lineage extinctions and population extinction. Valid but not automatically desirable content. A latched same-tick rupture takes precedence over population extinction. |
| **Limit draw** | Event, time, finite-packing or non-progress limit prevents a fully resolved terminal result. Explicit unresolved outcome/reason; no winner, containment or completed rupture is fabricated. |
| **Invalid** | Non-finite state, severe/unrecoverable penetration, impossible initial placement, invalid ancestry/radius/bounds, or another invariant failure. Preserve diagnostic reason and failed state; do not render as content. |

An opening **locks the rupture mode**, not permission to exceed the 18 s hard
ceiling. If that ceiling interrupts its .60 s release, record an unresolved draw
with `locked_outcome=rupture` and `release_incomplete=true`; preserve the real
opening and any real escapes, and never emit `rupture_resolved`. This distinguishes
a genuine wall failure from a falsely completed release. No post-rupture compression
or alternate successful outcome is possible. Late completed rupture is possible
if its opening occurs after 16.8 s but its release completes by 18 s.

## Narrow semantic log contract

No shared event framework. Frozen result/event records contain immutable JSON
payloads; initial state, every committed 120 Hz sample, and semantic before/after
states reconstruct the run without re-executing rules in a renderer. Every event
has run/sequence identity, tick, absolute time, kind, payload, before and after.
Ball identity, lineage, ancestry, geometry, contact decomposition, clocks, area,
mode, outcome/reason and eligibility remain inspectable. Non-finite rejected
numbers serialize as explicit diagnostic strings, never repaired numeric state.

| Event | Meaning and audit payload |
| --- | --- |
| `press_engaged` | Initial schedule/rules identity and first contact if present |
| `compression_advanced` | Schedule knot, first contact, or stall status change; actual/command state |
| `ball_strained` | Normal/moderate/extreme band transition and C/D/Q/L |
| `ball_split` | Qualified parent, two birth/relaxed children, lineage/generation, overlap and area ratio |
| `ball_crushed` | Qualified ball, latched cohort, duration, removed area and resulting relief |
| `wall_strained` | Panel strain-band transition and contact IDs/W |
| `wall_ruptured` | Latched W/duration, opening, release IDs, reset clocks and stopped piston |
| `ball_escaped` | Identity, actual crossing and previous position, opening, live/escaped counts |
| `chamber_contained` | Final stable state and 72-interval hold |
| `lineage_extinct` | No living members of that lineage |
| `simulation_draw` | Unresolved limit reason and retained state |
| `simulation_invalid` | Invariant failure, diagnostic state and reason |
| `population_extinct` | Zero living descendants after genuine crushing |
| `rupture_resolved` | Exactly 72 release intervals, real escaped IDs and retained survivors |

Capacity blocking is visible in live count, disabled clocks and samples; it does
not require redundant per-ball events. Continuous geometry/drive belongs in tick
samples, not a high-frequency stream of redundant semantic notifications.
