# MS06 — Pressure Multiplication

## Authorization and research question

The owner approves the procedural spectacle format-family direction. Operation
Inception is becoming a procedural spectator-format pipeline, not exclusively a
Chain Reaction generator. **MS04.4 remains the first genuine rules-format proof.**
The second format will explore pressure, compression, and ball multiplication,
inspired by hydraulic-press tension but with an original stylized mechanism and
visual identity. “Pressure Multiplication” is a working format name, not a final
brand or an instruction to reproduce an existing press animation.

> Can visible compression, limited space and rule-driven multiplication create
> an immediately understandable 12–20-second spectacle that generates
> anticipation, escalation and a satisfying resolution across many deterministic
> variations?

This is a research question, not an established audience effect. The format may
be nonphysical, but its internal rules must be clear and consistent. No outcome
may be secretly rewritten by rendering or sound. Several future formats may
create different feels; shared architecture is deferred until at least two
formats exist and this second format is creatively accepted.

**This turn is documentation only.** No simulation, video, generated outputs,
dependency installation, or generalized plugin framework is authorized. MS06.1
is specification work only. Do not begin MS06.2 until the owner approves the
MS06.1 rule objectives. Existing particle outputs, implementations, and MS04.4
remain untouched; this direction does not approve any published asset or master.

## First-frame promise and timing objectives

- A populated vertical chamber is already visible; no empty setup or slow reveal.
- A press or other compression boundary is visibly approaching.
- Available space is obviously limited, so the danger reads without text.
- Two or three ball identities are immediately distinguishable and offer
  something to follow. Whether they are teams or other identities is still open.
- Meaningful compression begins within approximately **0.5 seconds**.
- The first multiplication or major rule-driven state change occurs within
  approximately **1.5 seconds**.
- The run aims for **12–20 seconds**, including enough time to understand the rule,
  anticipate repeated consequences, notice a reversal, and read the ending.

These are research/selection targets, not permission to force a birth, rescue,
winner, or ending at a chosen time. A parameter set that cannot produce a genuine,
readable ending within the window needs revision or candidate rejection, not a
renderer-authored resolution. Timing targets also do not prove an opening is
understood; that requires a first-view phone judgment.

## MS06.1 — Format and rule specification

**Deliverable:** an owner-reviewable rule card, not code or a motion prototype.
Keep the first proposed mechanism small: one chamber, one compression mechanism,
two or three identities, one primary multiplication rule, one explicit source of
reversal, and one primary ending with honest unresolved/failure paths. Do not
implement every alternative below.

The following are **open owner decisions**, not approved defaults:

| Decision | What the specification must settle |
| --- | --- |
| Chamber geometry and safe areas | Shape, initial occupied region, usable clearance, visible capacity/boundary cues, and bottom/right phone-safe composition. Do not assume the MS04 grid layout fits this format. |
| Compression motion | One boundary or several; continuous, stepped, or feedback-driven approach; initial gap, speed changes, stopping condition, and whether a release phase is permitted. Distinguish the rule schedule from how it is drawn. |
| Identity and ownership | Are colors competing owners, stable ball families, or different behaviors? Can identity change? Define inheritance and any conversion before using color dominance as an ending. |
| Meaning of pressure | Choose one primary internal measure, such as normalized occupied area, local crowding, gap/contact compression, or accumulated compression exposure. Define units and locality. It is a declared game quantity, not a real hydraulic pressure reading. |
| Multiplication trigger | Specify an exact threshold/contact/exposure condition, when it is evaluated, required duration if any, cooldown, and deterministic tie/order policy. An attractive collision alone must not authorize an unexplained birth. |
| Multiplication transformation | Does a parent survive and add a child, disappear and become several children, or undergo another explicit transformation? State the exact population change, child identities, birth locations, IDs/lineage, and resource/cooldown consequences. |
| Space, capacity, and collision semantics | Decide whether overlap is forbidden or an explicit stylized state, how movement/contact is resolved, what counts as full, and what happens when a required birth has no legal space. Rendering must not silently reposition, merge, or delete balls to make a birth fit. |
| Runaway bounds | Choose the minimum explicit bounds needed: population/capacity, generation depth, per-step birth budget, cooldown, or another stated limit. Define the result of hitting a bound; do not silently discard excess multiplication. |
| Reversal | Choose a visible rule-driven change in danger, growth, survival, or control: for example, permitted relief, resource exhaustion/recovery, a capacity transfer, or a declared identity interaction. Define its cause. It must change the expectation rather than merely slow monotonic growth; no hidden last-second rescue. |
| Genuine ending | Choose one primary resolution: containment, overflow, chamber failure, color dominance, survival, or another explicit outcome. Define the exact predicate, when it becomes terminal, and what visible state proves it. No success/failure interpretation should depend on an added caption or decorative flash. |
| Draws, invalid runs, and non-termination | Define the time/event/step bounds and any repeated-state handling; decide which unresolved conditions are draws and which indicate invalid computation. A legitimate chamber-failure ending is different from an engine failure. Neither may be relabeled as a win or hidden by a cut. |
| Intentional stylization | List what is authored or nonphysical: pressure proxy, motion/contact model, splitting, identity behavior, chamber response, and timing. Do not imply hydraulic engineering or material realism. |

An approved rule card must make the same starting state and seed unambiguous:
given the compression schedule and a sequence of threshold/contact facts, a
reader can determine births, identities, capacity effects, reversal, and ending.
Seed variation may change approved initial conditions or declared random choices;
it must not bypass rules to obtain a desired outcome.

**Smallest recommended MS06.1 package:** one concise rule card containing the
chamber/composition and timing objectives, the selected transition rules, limits,
and a terminal-outcome matrix, plus three short hand-worked traces: an ordinary
multiplication, a crowded/reversal case, and a terminal or unresolved-limit case.
These are written examples, not a simulation or generated review asset. Explicit
owner approval of that package is the entry gate to MS06.2. Unchosen alternatives
stay outside the first implementation.

## MS06.2 — Deterministic simulation

**Entry gate:** approved MS06.1 rule objectives and decisions. Not authorized now.

Plan a format-specific, seeded rules engine. Reproducible runs must emit an
immutable semantic event log and preserve state changes, lineage/identity,
boundary movement, pressure transitions, multiplication or blocked births,
reversal causes, and terminal/draw/failure reasons. Exact event names and data
structures belong to the later approved implementation, not a shared framework
designed now.

Resolve simultaneous actions in the approved stable order. Rules must not depend
on rendering FPS, audio, visual contact effects, or a request for a particular
ending. Rendering consumes recorded truth; audio consumes corresponding timed
events. Both must be unable to alter the run. Tests should target the approved
transitions, capacity/bounds, repeatability, and honest failure paths, not visual
smoothness as a substitute for correctness.

**Exit evidence:** deterministic valid traces and genuine terminal outcomes under
the approved rules, including explicit unsuccessful and unresolved examples.
No creative acceptance follows from those checks alone.

## MS06.3 — Visual and sound prototype

Plan **one** short phone-safe vertical prototype, not a batch of polished pacing
variants or a publishing master. Start already populated with an approaching
boundary and visible limited space. Make compression escalate, multiplication
legible at normal speed, the supported identity/object/outcome trackable, and the
genuine ending decisive. Establish an original graphic mechanism and visual
identity rather than copying hydraulic-press references or Chain Reaction assets.

No headline, explanatory text, score counter, narration, captions, or music
integration belongs inside the generated video. Spoken hooks, captions, and
TikTok music remain external production choices. Procedural event effects may
reinforce compression, splitting, reversal, and resolution, but must not provide
an explanation or payoff missing from the image.

Use the smallest format-specific presentation path justified by the approved
simulation. Existing timing/export/audio practices may be evaluated for direct
reuse; do not refactor MS04.4 or invent a universal format interface to do so.
Review at normal speed before polishing. No artifact is generated in MS06.1.

## MS06.4 — Batch generation and selection

After a working single-run prototype, plan seeded generation of multiple genuine
candidates under controlled parameters. Record seeds, parameters, complete
outcomes, and selection reasons, including failures/draws. Compare variations
without silently changing rules or choosing a winner in the renderer.

| Transparent selection proxy | Intended question; limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Time to first meaningful transformation | Does the run meet the early-change target? A timestamp does not establish comprehension. |
| Increasing occupied-space pressure | Does the approved pressure measure escalate? Occupancy alone does not prove felt tension. |
| Multiplication escalation | Do births form readable escalating consequences rather than an instant untrackable burst? Raw count is insufficient. |
| Reversal or threatened failure | Is there a recorded change in danger or expected outcome? A numerical crossing does not prove surprise. |
| Color balance and changing dominance | Where identities support this meaning, is there something to favor and a real change in control? Do not invent ownership for purely decorative colors. |
| Peak event intensity | Is there a concentrated high point within the legibility budget? Maximum density is not automatically better. |
| Late uncertainty | Does the rules/state trajectory leave a late unresolved possibility? This is a heuristic, not a viewer-prediction measurement. |
| Clear terminal resolution | Does the approved terminal predicate become visibly distinguishable? A status field does not prove a decisive ending. |

These are engineering proxies, not proof of audience satisfaction, retention, or
replay interest. Select genuine candidates; never conceal a draw as containment,
manufacture a comeback, or render an outcome that the run did not produce.

## MS06.5 — Format-family compatibility

**Entry gate:** the second format exists and is creatively accepted. Direction
approval alone is not this gate. At least two concrete formats must exist first.

Compare the MS04 Chain Reaction-inspired rules format with Pressure Multiplication
to identify genuinely shared needs: seeds, event identity, absolute timing,
rendering/export contracts, audio cues, manifests, and human review gates. Compare
actual differences too, including state, causality, capacity, and terminal logic.
Extract only demonstrated common components while retaining format-specific
truth. No generalized plugin system, speculative registry, or broad framework
belongs before this comparison.

## Creative acceptance: owner-and-wife phone review

Review once at normal speed, without an explanatory caption, a rule briefing, or
slow playback before answering. Record each person's observations separately.

1. Is the situation understood during the first second?
2. What does the viewer think will happen?
3. Is the multiplication rule understandable after seeing it once?
4. Does pressure visibly escalate?
5. Is there a specific object, color, or outcome to support?
6. Is there a moment when the expected outcome changes?
7. Does the ending feel earned and decisive?
8. Does the viewer want to watch another generated variation?
9. Does it feel like social content rather than a professional simulation demo?
10. Is the 12–20-second duration justified?

A useful review requires concrete answers about the danger, multiplication rule,
supported subject, reversal, and ending, not just “looks smooth.” If either viewer
needs an explanation to understand those, record the failure and revisit the
relevant rule or presentation decision before polish. If responses differ, keep
both; technical checks do not settle the disagreement. Approval of MS06.1 is a
rule/specification gate; creative acceptance of a later prototype is separate.
No MS06 viewing result is claimed now.

## Explicit non-goals

- Real hydraulic engineering, physically accurate material simulation, or
  instructions for operating real machinery.
- A general-purpose physics engine or generalized format/plugin framework.
- Photorealism or copying another format's branding, assets, or visual design.
- A publishing master or any publishing integration.
- Voiceover, spoken hooks, captions, or music integration in procedural output.
- Cloud services, dashboards, agents, LLMs, Hugging Face models, or generative
  visual models; no model is required.
- Reworking, overwriting, or regenerating frozen particle outputs or MS04.4.
- Simulation implementation, rendering, output generation, or dependency
  installation during this objectives/specification turn.
