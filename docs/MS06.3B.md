## Observed evidence

A and B transmit load through the bed but stall with maximum earned D=.723842
and .757324 respectively, below extreme-load qualification. Their 64-pass piston
trials roll back. Later commanded travel does not earn more drive, so crushing
cannot free the packing. A briefly reaches wall strain (maximum W=.633362), but
never the .80 rupture condition; B peaks at W=.567993.

C provides enough actual travel to reach D=1 and crush eight balls. That pressure
release is genuine, but wall stress remains zero. Its geometry does not transfer
load into the side-wall beads. D's wider, shallower close packing stalls even
earlier, at D=.389230, so it earns neither split nor crush qualification.

Evidence correction: the proposed closure wording “No tested setup generated
wall strain” conflicts with the preserved report. A logged **one** `wall_strained`
event at 2.366667 s, with peak W=.6333618164. It did not reach the .80 rupture
threshold. B, C and D logged no wall-strain events; the containment setup C had
no wall strain, rupture or escapes. No MS06.3B setup ruptured or escaped. The
immutable report is preserved, and this closure retains that distinction.

The compact beds stalled before sufficient load developed for crushing. The one
containment setup produced limited whole-bed motion: 68.75% displaced by 3 s,
84.375% before containment and 90.625% ever piston-connected. Meaningful load
reached the middle at 2.067 s (stored 2.066667 s) and lower third at 5.800 s. Only
one of six original lower-cohort balls moved and became meaningfully loaded.
No qualifying rupture base existed; the eight-probe audit was not run.

## Working inference — next research direction

PB-1.1 drive depends only on achieved travel after first contact. When resistance
prevents further achieved movement, drive stops increasing even while the
commanded piston continues downward. The working hypothesis is that an explicit,
bounded resistance/debt state could let a stylized hydraulic-style actuator build
load while blocked. This is a **PB-1.2 research hypothesis**, not evidence of real
hydraulic behavior or a retroactive change to PB-1.1.

These are coupled authoring/solver/load difficulties in the tested arrangements,
not permission to raise a renderer glow, insert cracks, open a wall, delete balls,
slow a conformance trace, lower thresholds, add hidden pressure or change the
terminal label. The result does not establish universal PB-1.1 impossibility.
The owner has selected versioned pressure-propagation research rather than
further PB-1.1 geometry tuning. No PB-1.2 formula or implementation is adopted
by this closure.
