# MS04.4 — Genuine-match end-to-end attention test

## Research question and status

Can the owner-approved grid, rules, automated match, procedural sound, and a
text-free vertical presentation produce a spectator game that is understandable
and worth continuing to watch on a phone?

The first complete review package is technically complete. It presents the
unaltered, owner-approved MS04.3 seed 4304 result and is pending normal-speed
owner-and-wife phone review. It is not approved for publication, and technical
decoding does not establish attention, comprehension, or satisfaction.

## Genuine match source

The source is `ms04.3.three-team.v1.seed-4304`: 87 legal turns, 2,601 immutable
logical events, and a genuine cyan victory produced by the MS04.2 rules. MS04.4
does not rerun policies, choose moves, or assign a winner. A deterministic
presentation mapper reconstructs every board state from the complete logical log;
its final state must equal the search record before rendering can proceed.

MS04.2 now emits an `orb_received` fact for each individual cascade transfer.
This adds presentation detail without changing any placement, cascade, ownership,
elimination, draw, or victory result. All 780 transfers in the selected match have
an adjacent source cell and an absolute source and arrival time.

## Opening and absolute clock

Live analytics from the earlier TikTok prototype reported 97 views, 4.4 seconds
average watch time, 1.2% full completion, and the largest loss at 0:01. This small
sample is not an audience verdict, but it is decisive evidence against a slow
intro. The MS04.4 review therefore has no pre-roll:

- the first orb lands at 0.035 seconds;
- the first three turns use 0.32 seconds each, revealing all three teams by
  0.675 seconds;
- turns 4–9 remain slower at 0.48 seconds so the rule can be observed;
- ordinary turns use a 0.20-second base, while cascades extend naturally to keep
  transfers ordered and readable;
- the genuine winner is declared at 38.178 seconds and the board holds unchanged
  for 1.60 seconds.

The exported duration is 39.80 seconds at 30 FPS. This is deliberately a complete
match, not proof that 39.8 seconds is an acceptable publishing length. Sustained
middle-section interest is the primary creative question.

## Presentation

The 720×1280 review has a phone-safe 6×9 board, no text, score, counter, labels,
explanatory UI, voiceover, or music. Ownership is communicated by saturated orb
color and a quieter cell tint. Multiple orbs have distinct positions within a
cell. Cascades use source-to-neighbor travel, capture pulses, and explosion rings;
the final all-cyan board gains a clear cyan perimeter and an unchanged resolution
hold. The renderer consumes the presentation plan only and cannot call the rules
engine or change game truth.

The sound adapter consumes the same genuine absolute timeline. It filters dense
redundancy while retaining placement, near-critical charge, critical state,
explosion, opponent capture, cascade depth, elimination, and victory cues. The
review contains 926 deterministic cues. Its 48 kHz stereo WAV peaks at −1.31 dBFS;
team voices are related but distinct. Perceived clarity, harshness, fatigue, and
team identity still require phone listening.

## Verification and boundaries

Both complete MP4s were fully decoded after export. The sound and muted copies are
720×1280, 30 FPS, 39.80 seconds, YUV 4:2:0, and explicitly tagged BT.709. Focused
checks cover genuine-source enforcement, complete reconstruction, transfer
adjacency and timing, audio filtering, deterministic rendering, and FPS-independent
presentation time.

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m inception.ms04_4 \
  --config configs/ms04.4.toml --output-dir outputs/ms04.4
.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_ms04_4.py
.venv/bin/python tools/verify_particle_freeze.py
```

The exporter refuses existing MS04.4 files unless `--overwrite` is explicitly
supplied; that option is scoped to this milestone directory. No 1080p master,
TikTok upload, publishing integration, narration, model, or generative service was
added. The planned “Trust your instinct. Pick a color.” hook remains external.
