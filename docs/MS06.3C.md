## Research and boundary

Can a populated, irregular two-lineage bed give an understandable 11–14-second
spectacle of whole-bed compression, readable multiplication, crush-based reversal,
wall strain and genuine rupture/escape? This was a directed scenario, not an
automatically selected candidate. No event time, pressure history or outcome was
injected. There is no renderer or sound adapter in this slice.

The unchanged PB-1.2 engine uses 120 Hz active steps, gravity pre-settling,
derived compression debt, section-specific wall stress and latched
crush → rupture → split ordering. Its split/crush/rupture clocks remain
0.30/0.15/0.40 seconds. See [MS06.2B](MS06.2B.md) for the versioned rules.

## Numerical integrity

All recorded values remained finite. Maximum unresolved penetration was
.005242767 world units, below the severe-overlap amplitude threshold of half the
smallest radius. However, overlap persisted across 31 observations spanning
30 ticks (.25 seconds), reaching the existing unresolved-duration ceiling. The
explicit draw is therefore a failed numerical gate, not containment or rupture.
Non-progress counters remained zero because the final commanded position had
not yet been scheduled; this was the separate packing-stall limit.

Maximum stored speed was 6.719774 units/s. Maximum observed displacement speed
for a persistent identity was 15.355925 units/s; constraint corrections can differ
from stored velocities. These are risks for later readable motion, not polish
opportunities around the failed gate.

The maximum commanded schedule rate (.275 units/s) stayed below the unchanged
.4 units/s movement policy. There was no command-policy rejection or invalid
result. The log records a `piston_blocked` episode due to an unresolved constraint
trial. Detailed per-attempt rollback counts were **not collected** in this vanilla
run and are null in the report, not asserted to be zero.

At the first positive-debt sample, achieved travel was .000000238 and commanded
travel .002291667; derived debt was .002291429 and D .008056641. At the draw,
achieved travel was .026560731, commanded travel .217758333, debt .191197602
and D .765556641. These histories are derived from recorded positions. The
commanded piston was at .704595244; achieved position was .896833111.

An additional **observational** nonnegative contact-equilibrium diagnostic tested
the local reaction path when debt became positive. It did not obtain a certificate
within 30,000 iterations: residual .157340630 versus tolerance .001. This bounded
numerical search does not prove equilibrium impossible, physical realism or an
engine bug, and its forces never affect simulation state. It supplies no positive
evidence that the early resistance was fully supported by the recorded contacts.
The independent overlap-duration failure already requires stopping.
