# MS06.3 — Directed Pressure Bloom phone prototype

## Research question and status

Can PB-1.1 produce an understandable 14–18-second spectacle with immediate
compression, readable multiplication, escalating crowding, genuine crush-based
release, a decisive ending, and local contact robustness?

This is **one directed PB-1.1 prototype**, not an automatically generated candidate.
Its initial geometry and piston schedule are authored inputs. Every birth, crush,
escape and terminal state is determined by the unchanged MS06.2 engine. No event
timestamp or result is scripted. Creative acceptance remains **owner-and-wife
normal-speed phone review pending**. MS06.4, publishing and a 1080p master remain
unauthorized.

## Contact-robustness gate

Freeze the base before running eight predetermined nearby probes. These are an
audit, not seed search, selection, or MS06.4. No favorable probe replaces the base:

- Alternating ball X offsets ±.0015 (two probes).
- Alternating ball Y offsets ±.0015 (two probes).
- All piston schedule times scaled for97% and 103% speed (two probes).
- Vertical spacing about the mean Y increased .3% (one probe).
- Post-zero schedule knots delayed by exactly one 120 Hz tick (one probe).

Each case runs twice and compares complete immutable results, not only terminal
labels. `ms06.3-robustness.json` records exact perturbed configurations, parameters,
engine/configuration hashes, all semantic events, full final states, timing/count
summaries, phase measurements, simulation/event hashes and replay equality.
Four local processes bound this fixed audit's runtime; there is no ranking,
candidate generator, agent or generalized format infrastructure.

## Simulation, presentation and audio boundaries

- `ms06_3_scenario.py` owns this explicit setup/audit only; it calls the unchanged
  PB-1.1 engine before any presentation.
- `ms06_3.py` freezes a hash-verified timeline, then exports from it. The
  `--render-frozen` path does not call simulation or transition logic.
- `pressure_presentation.py` imports no rules/audio module. Its frozen tape stores
  immutable states/events; continuous pose is interpolated between120 Hz samples.
  Membership follows committed events: children never precede birth, active
  crushed balls never survive removal, and escapes remain genuine recorded IDs.
- `pressure_audio.py` imports no engine or renderer. Immutable semantic events
  supply absolute seconds, independent of FPS. Split/crush cues retain source IDs;
  low-priority strain chatter is coalesced into .18-second bins. Every real split,
  crush, wall rupture and terminal resolution remains individually mapped.

The camera is fixed. A dark original chamber, broad descending compression head,
saturated lineage fills and strong outlines emphasize the beads. The chamber
spans x90..530, floor y=1050 and original top y=82 in 720×1280 pixels, leaving right
and bottom interface clearance. Load deforms drawn ellipses only; collision
geometry is unchanged. Clock-driven strain rings precede logged transitions;
splits use quick stretch/connector art, crushes use compact collapse art. These
are effects tied to genuine events, not new living balls. Wall flex/cracks require
recorded wall strain; only a recorded ruptured panel can open. This base has no
wall-strain event and therefore invents no cracks or breakage. A stable sealed
boundary/head response marks actual containment.

Sound is procedural 48 kHz stereo: low engagement/movement, quiet rising strain,
higher split pops with related lineage pitch identities, heavier crush impacts,
wall creak/rupture snap and rush when applicable, and a quiet containment interval.
Absolute event times share the visual clock. Seeded noise is local per cue. Shared
master attenuation controls overlap peaks; there is no independent per-cue
limiter that changes event timing. WAV finite/peak/mono measurements, cue mapping,
PCM hash and master gain are recorded. Sound distinction and comfort remain
phone-listening questions. No narration, hook, captions, music, external samples,
model or audio service is involved.

## Verification and limits of evidence

Focused checks cover frozen hash enforcement and missing ticks, event-controlled
identity membership, renderer independence, deterministic selected frames,
absolute event-to-audio mapping, reproducible finite/unclipped PCM, deterministic
nearby-probe construction/replay, gate rejection, timeline/manifest agreement,
all file hashes, 720×1280/30 FPS, explicit BT.709, same encoded video in both copies,
and full decode of both MP4s. Full repository tests and preservation checks also
run; prior particle, MS04, MS05 and MS06.2 artifacts remain byte-identical.

Completed verification: **8 focused tests and 90 full-suite tests passed**.
Both MP4s fully decode and share the same encoded H.264 stream. Audio has 176
cues, 48 kHz stereo, shared master gain .9404723 and peak **−1.31 dBFS**. All six
manifest-listed artifact hashes and all recorded source/configuration hashes
match. Frozen verification confirms 34 outputs and 15 legacy implementation/config
files; all five MS04.4 artifact hashes match. A pre-turn inventory also confirms
all pre-existing output bytes, including MS05 and MS06.2, remain unchanged.
Whitespace and changed-file scope checks pass.

Local robustness does not establish format repeatability across arbitrary seeds.
The containment endpoint may feel quieter than rupture, and all three lineages
remain. Twenty original lower balls never divide; the pressure activity is
concentrated higher in the chamber. Whole-chamber occupied fraction rises from
about36.9% initially to about 44.3%, then ends at 43.2%; live-count growth is not a
claim that all free space disappears. Four early roots split close together,
which may be less readable than a single isolated demonstration. Dense later
sound may fatigue or mask individual events despite peak safety. Projection
limits can still cause valid stalls or explicit draws in other inputs.

Review must establish first-second comprehension, rule clarity, felt escalation,
reversal comprehension, earned containment, replay interest and whether the
length is justified. Technical success and nearby-probe validity establish none
of these. Owner creative review remains open; **do not begin MS06.4 or a
publication master without another owner approval**.
