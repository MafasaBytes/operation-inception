# Vision: procedural spectator formats

The owner approves Operation Inception's direction as a deterministic procedural
spectator-format pipeline for vertical social video, not exclusively a Chain
Reaction generator. Several formats may eventually create different feels.
MS03B closes the particle phase as a successful competitive-color concept proof.
Its useful mechanism was immediate color allegiance, visible competition, reversible
control, threatened elimination, a decisive comeback, and a winner communicated
through color. That owner decision is the basis for the pivot; it is not a claim
of universal audience response or physical realism.

The product hypothesis is that a viewer remains engaged when a situation is
immediately readable, there is a team, object, or outcome to support, pressure or
risk escalates, expectations can reverse, and the ending is understandable
without explanatory text. Competition is one route; compression, limited space,
and rule-driven multiplication are another proposed route. Each format must obey
clear internal rules even when stylized and nonphysical. Select genuine runs;
the renderer must never secretly rewrite an outcome or fabricate a winner.

The first rules format uses the owner-approved 6×9 portrait rectangular board
from MS04.1. MS04.4 remains the first genuine rules-format proof. Chain Reaction
supplies a psychological reference only.
Develop an original name, visual identity, presentation, and eventually rule
variations. Do not copy its branding, interfaces, assets, sounds, or visual design.
The project has not selected a final game name or established a visual identity.

Keep game state, presentation timing, rendering, and audio logically separate.
MS04.2 implements pure rules and logical events; MS05 establishes procedural
sound from separate synthetic timed events. MS04.3 now performs seeded move
automation and genuine-result selection. MS04.4 supplies the narrow absolute-time
mapper, event-only renderer, and genuine-match sound adapter. Its copied review
is an experiment awaiting human acceptance, not a publication master. Only the
MS04.1 topology dependency is included here; its static exporter/package was not
transferred. The detailed milestone boundaries are in [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md).

The second proposed format is **MS06 — Pressure Multiplication**: visible
compression and ball multiplication in limited space, inspired by hydraulic-press
tension but using an original stylized mechanism and visual identity. Its target
is an immediately understandable 12–20-second spectacle across deterministic
variations. Only format/rule objectives and specification are authorized now;
MS06.2 implementation requires owner approval of the MS06.1 rule objectives.
See [MS06](MS06.md) for open decisions and the phone-review gate.

Keep the two formats concrete and separate first. Extract shared architecture
only after at least two formats exist and the second is creatively accepted.
Do not design a generalized plugin framework in anticipation of that comparison.
The direction approval does not change the acceptance/publication status of any
existing output or authorize reworking MS04.4.

No existing output is approved for publication. All Experiment 001, 001B, MS03,
and MS03B artifacts and implementations are frozen. MS03B must not be polished,
regenerated, overwritten, or exported at 1080p. Its successful concept proof is
not permission to publish it. No 1080p MS03B master was produced or requested.

The active scope excludes publishing integrations, TikTok APIs, cloud services,
dashboards, agents, LLMs, Hugging Face models, and generative visual models; none
is required for these formats. Spoken hooks, captions, and TikTok music remain
outside the procedural rendering pipeline. The social hook “Trust your instinct.
Pick a color.” is external production material to record or add later in TikTok
Studio, not part of the rendering or procedural audio system. Procedural event
effects are separate from voiceover or a music-track integration.

[Historical particle-phase vision](archive/VISION-particle-phase.md) is retained
as a closed research record, not a competing active direction.
