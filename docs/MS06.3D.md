## PB-2.0 capacity contract

This is explicitly stylized and nonphysical. Absolute time advances at 120 Hz.
Width W and scheduled usable piston height H give area A=W×H. With efficiency
η, effective capacity K=A×η, occupied area O=Σπr² for inside balls and R=O/K.
No circle position, packing solver or layout failure participates in these rules.
Piston height follows a finite, non-increasing piecewise-linear schedule.

Each ball carries persistent ID, lineage, parent, generation, radius, resistance
and four logical wall affinities. SHA-256 of version/seed/ID/generation produces
a resistance u in [0,1), without lineage input. Four separate hash values produce
positive normalized affinities near 1/4. Children inherit lineage, advance g,
append `.0`/`.1` IDs, use .75r and derive their own recorded resistance/affinities.
Initial ancestry excerpts may start above g0 but must state this explicitly.

At moderate R a ball sustains its individual split threshold for the qualification
duration. A global cadence admits **one** qualified parent per interval. Select
by completed qualification tick, resistance, ID. A split replaces its parent
atomically; child area is 1.125×parent area. Generation≤3 and live≤96 are hard
bounds. Cap/generation blocking never deletes a ball or fabricates an outcome.

At high R an individual crush threshold and sustained duration qualify a ball.
Global crush cadence admits one in completion-tick/resistance/ID order. Crushing
removes real occupied area, recomputes R and records its actual reduction.
Lineage is excluded from eligibility and selection. Qualification clocks reset
when the full condition fails. Growth can therefore create later vulnerability.

Four stable wall sections are L0/L1 (lower/upper left) and R0/R1 (lower/upper
right), spanning half the initial usable height each. Logical assigned area
O_s=Σ(area_i×affinity_i,s). Section stress is

    W_s = clamp(max(0, R−1) / wall_pressure_span × (4 O_s/O) × weakness_s, 0, 1)

Zero occupied area gives zero stress. All terms and ball contributions are
recorded. The fixed section weakness is part of the input before simulation.
Strain starts at .60; .80 sustained for .40 s qualifies rupture. This exposure
clock supplies the sustained component; no arbitrary pressure history is injected.

Use one immutable pre-transition capacity snapshot each tick. Advance clocks,
latch qualification, apply crush → rupture → split, then validate. Simultaneous
rupture sections sort by completion tick then section ID. Same-tick crushing
cannot cancel an already-qualified rupture. Structural event payloads include
before/after capacity and identity records. Sequence numbers are contiguous.

Rupture locks its actual failed section, freezes the piston, resets qualification
clocks and stops all new crush/split/rupture qualification. Live inside balls whose
largest affinity belongs to that section form a deterministic discharge queue,
ordered by descending failed-section affinity then ID. The release policy emits
one real `ball_escaped` per discharge interval after an initial delay; only those
actual live identities change status. Escape is an explicit logical discharge
rule, not a claim of physical circle crossing. Rendering visualizes the recorded
queue/crossing time and cannot add escapes. Other survivors remain inside.

Terminal outcomes are mutually exclusive: rupture after its release interval;
containment at final piston position with no pending enabled transitions for .60s;
extinction after all balls are crushed; explicit event/time/non-progress limit
draw; invalid for non-finite/invalid inputs or violated invariants. An incomplete
release at a hard ceiling draws rather than masquerading as completed rupture.
Maxima remain g3, 96 live, 20,000 events and 18 seconds. Layout failure is a
separate presentation failure and cannot change any of these outcomes.

## Frozen boundaries and presentation

The rules result consists of immutable canonical JSON strings. Before rendering,
the complete result, semantic log, layout frames, configuration, constants and
source hashes are frozen. `capacity_presentation` imports neither rules nor layout
logic. It validates contiguous 120 Hz ticks, event sequence/time, every displayed
identity/radius/lineage/generation/status against logical state, finite positions
and all hashes. At 30 FPS it samples frozen layout/states; after terminal time it
holds the final displayed pose. It never advances the engine.

Layout uses seeded irregular initial positions, deterministic soft relaxation,
whole-bed display compression and load-driven flattening. It does not simulate
physical pressure. Radius remains an unchanged logical property; flattened
ellipses are visual deformation. The smallest nominal display radius exceeds
8px. A ball larger than the display chamber, non-finite pose or inaccessible
opening is a separate presentation failure, not a gameplay draw. Forced failure
is tested to leave rule truth unchanged.

The actual discharge queue determines which identities approach the opening.
The layout maps each logged escape to a short approach and outward trajectory;
the renderer does not invent a contact, create an escape or choose its time.
This abstraction is intentionally distinct from physically derived circle escapes.
Walls receive stress from logical affinities, not visual contact positions.

The chamber spans x64..624 (560 pixels, about86% of a 650 px phone-safe width),
with floor at 1080 and the populated opening at about 531. The broad compression
head and central guide fill the remaining vertical composition. Colors are
saturated cyan/orange, with thick edges and a quiet background. Actual split
events supply parent bulge, child-pair connector/dumbbell and separation art.
Crushes compact only genuinely removed identities. Recorded section stress drives
flex/cracks; L0 alone opens after its real rupture. There is no text/UI, score,
narration, machinery branding or decorative population mistaken for real balls.
